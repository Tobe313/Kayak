How it works

  • Each slot holds up to 0.74 s, mono, at 22 050 Hz: drag on the wave to choose which part is kept.
  • On the FM-1: EDIT, GRN SRC A (or B), turn TYPE past METAL to SMP1, SMP2… TONE under 64 darkens it, MOVE moves its start, SEED 64 and up plays it backwards.
  • ROOT is the note the sound has: played on that key, it keeps its pitch.
  • While a slot is written the sound stops for about half a second.
  • Needs Kayak 1.2 or later. Chrome asks once to allow MIDI with “system exclusive messages”.
